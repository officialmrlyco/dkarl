Dear Esther

Some friendships do not arrive with noise. They start quietly, then become a place you remember.

Essy, I met you in 2019, but back then we barely talked. It was almost funny because we feared each other for no real reason. Then early 2020 came, we started talking, and within weeks we moved from strangers to best friends so quickly it still feels surprising.

You are stubborn in that baby way, childish in a way that can make someone smile, and dramatic enough to be disappointed over a five-second missed ring call. But behind all that is a kind heart. You do not even keep anger for long, and somehow you can still be friendly even when someone has annoyed you.

I think that is one of the things that makes you rare. You are beautiful, yes, in a way that is hard to ignore, but it is also the way you accept your mistakes, the way you laugh, the way you become easy to talk to, and the way your presence can make ordinary days feel lighter.

I will not say everything too directly here. Some things are better left as a small mystery. But if you ever read between the lines and feel like this page is saying there might be something more hidden inside the friendship, maybe you are not completely wrong.

You are special to me, Essy. In a way I still want to understand slowly.
